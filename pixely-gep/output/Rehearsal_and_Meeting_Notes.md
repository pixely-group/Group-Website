Founded on **1 December 2025** means **9 months and 15 days** on the event date. Say “founded December 2025” or “less than a year ago,” rather than “10 months.”

## Programme fit

The invitation confirms attendance, not GEP admission. The current official GEP page, updated 27 August 2026, targets overseas scaling businesses establishing a UK HQ, subsidiary or R&D centre. It lists revenue starting at £250,000, sector dependent, growth, a successful equity raise, registered IP, expanding organisational capacity, and founder relocation or a senior strategic UK role. Your materials do not establish all those criteria. Ask the delegation how they apply to this cohort and your stage.

British-Algerian identity strengthens your founder story. It does not establish company eligibility. If the company is too early for GEP, ask for the appropriate UK investment-support route and the milestones for reconsideration.

Good morning. I’m Amine Laissoub, a British and Algerian founder. I founded Pixely in Algeria in December 2025, after working on digital financial products in the UK.

Pixely is building a platform that brings together payments, digital identity and AI services. Our starting point is a familiar financial journey: prove who you are, make a payment and know whether it succeeded. Delays, interruptions and identity fraud can undermine that journey.

The platform combines verification, payment workflows and risk intelligence through modular services and APIs. For AI, the proposed first pilot is fraud review. Use permitted identity and payment signals to flag unusual patterns for a human reviewer. Measure results against an agreed baseline, including false alerts and missed fraud. That gives a partner a concrete way to evaluate the technology.

## Questions to rehearse

**What is live today?**
Name the exact feature, user environment and date. Show one working flow if available. Separate simulated transactions from real-money transactions. If still in development: “We are currently at [actual stage]. The next proof point is [specific test].” Fill these facts privately before the meeting.

**Who pays you, and how much revenue do you have?**
Lead with the actual numbers and period, even if zero. Then explain the buyer and next commercial milestone. Never answer a traction question with a market-size estimate.

**What makes your AI defensible?**
Explain the task, data rights, baseline, evaluation and feedback process. Identify what you own versus third-party models or services. An API integration alone does not demonstrate proprietary model performance. If evaluation is pending, state the planned test.

**Why the UK?**
Name the work that would happen there: commercial leadership, a defined research collaboration or partner development. Explain the next customer or capability it helps secure. Do not invent prospective partners or promise headcount without a funded hiring plan.

**Why you?**
Use your British-Algerian background, one specific UK product example and the team's relevant capability. Explain what you personally delivered.

**Are you authorised to handle payments?**
Describe Pixely’s actual role and current status. Identify partners and documented permissions where applicable. Avoid “fully compliant” without evidence. If a review is pending, say what is under review and how deployment depends on it.

**How do offline payments avoid double spending or losses?**
Explain the actual mechanism, transaction limits, reconciliation and liability arrangements, supported by tests. If those are not ready, describe offline payments as a development objective, not a production capability.

**Do you meet the GEP criteria?**
“We would like you to assess our fit against our current evidence. If we are earlier than the programme’s present target, what milestones should we meet, and which UK support route is appropriate now?” Add real revenue, funding and IP facts when known.

**How much funding are you raising?**
If you have a defined raise, connect the amount to runway and measurable milestones. If not: “Today’s request is a scoping session and relevant partner introductions. We will size funding against the validated pilot and expansion plan.”

**Is this an Algeria–UK remittance product?**
Only say yes if it is actually your product. The supplied materials describe a platform; they do not establish a cross-border remittance service. Explain your current use case rather than allowing the audience to assume one.
